"Wild or Pet?" Game

What is "Wild or Pet?"
"Wild or Pet?" Is an educational runner game where players learn to identify wild animals and pets.
The player controls a girl who must take the correct choice when an animal appears.
Wild animal → Jump over it
Pet animal → Touch it
Correct answer → Gain a point
Wrong answer → Lose a point

Why Did I create this game?
Some animals can look cute, friendly, or harmless, but that does not mean they are safe to keep as pets.
My goal is to help players understand the difference between wild animals and domesticated pets.
The game also shows that some pets can be useful to people when they are properly cared for.
Examples:
Cow → milk
Chicken → eggs
Dog → companionship
Sheep → wool

Animals in my game
30 Animals
Wild Animals:
Capybara, Chimpanzee, Crocodile, Fox, Hedgehog, Lion, Otter, Owl, Raccoon, Skunk, Snake, Tiger, Wolf, Turtle
Pet Animals:
Camel, Cat, Chameleon, Chicken, Cow, Dog, Duck, Fish, Goat, Hamster, Horse, Parrot, Rabbit, Rooster, Sheep

How to play
1. Click PLAY to start the game.
2. Watch for animals appearing on the road.
3. If the animals is WILD, press SPACE to jump.
4. It the animal is a PET, touch the animal.
5. Make a correct choice to earn points.
Try to get enough points to WIN.
6. If you lose all your health/points, the game ends.

My Research
I researched the difference between wild animals and domesticated animals.
I learned that an animal being cute or small does not automatically make it a good pet.
Wild animals have natural behaviors and needs that can make them difficult or unsafe to keep around people.
Domesticated animals have been bred over generations to live with humans and can provide companionship, food, or other benefits.
The sites that helped me decide which animals to include in my game:

The Difference Between Domesticated and Wild Animals - Wildlife Informer
